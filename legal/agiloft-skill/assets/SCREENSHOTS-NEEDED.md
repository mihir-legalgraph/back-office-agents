## Before publishing

**Check every screenshot for real contract data.** These go in a repo. Counterparty
names, contract titles and IDs will be visible in tables and chart legends.

Either run against the test knowledgebase, or blur the counterparty column and
contract titles before committing. The tile figures and chart shapes are fine —
it's the record-level tables that carry names.
